Our Story

Founded in Botswana with a passion for fragrance.

OwenScents was founded by Owen Koketso, a Motswana entrepreneur with a vision to make premium fragrances more accessible without losing the feeling of luxury. The store was created for people who want authentic perfumes that match their mood, confidence, elegance, and personal expression.

From fresh everyday scents to bold evening fragrances, OwenScents helps customers discover perfumes that feel personal, memorable, and refined.
